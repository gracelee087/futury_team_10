## Platform
web
## Stack
Static HTML/CSS/JavaScript prototypes, confirmed by the user. Backend integration is outside this artifact.
## Users
ITSG reviewers assessing software results, confirmed by the user.
## Product Purpose
SÜSIE — Systemüberprüfung sozialversicherungsrechtlicher IT-Ergebnisse. Organize evidence and support a traceable human review of payroll-software results.
## Operating Context
Sources → requirements → human-reviewed criteria → synthetic test cases → observed execution → comparison → report and human review.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Show source citations, review states, expected and observed values, and missing evidence. AI suggestions remain candidates. No certification or legal-compliance claims. Prototypes use synthetic data and simulated decisions only.
## Brand Commitments
Name and expansion confirmed by the user. Three alternative design concepts requested: Prüfakte, Evidenzdesk, Prüfpfad. User selected Prüfakte (design 1). Audience: experienced German legal/domain reviewers. Current request replaces the blue-gray skin with two neutral themes, Casual and High Contrast, both with light and dark variants. ITSG crimson remains a small wordmark accent.
## Evidence on Hand
Repository plan.md, itsg/records.py, itsg/states.py and synthetic fixtures. Prototype values are explicitly illustrative.
## Open Decisions
Prüfakte selected. Production framework remains undecided.

## Current logo and review behavior
The user requested a ChatGPT-generated SÜSIE logo. The final transparent PNG is integrated at assets/susie-logo.png, with a white rendering in dark variants. Notes and drafts are kept in browser-tab session storage and can be exported in the synthetic JSON report; they do not approve findings.
